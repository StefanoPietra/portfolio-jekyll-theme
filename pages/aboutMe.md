# Something about myself

My name is Stefano, I’m from Italy and I’ve been living in London for a few years.

My background is in life sciences, I’ve studied cell and molecular biology and worked as a postdoctoral researcher in academia. After deciding to transition towards a career in data science, I’ve started to learn Python on my own with free online resources and then decided to pursue a certificate in data analytics to strengthen my foundations and gather more hands-on coding experience.

Among my recent projects there's one to automate scraping board game prices from specialist websites and one to systematically build a map of South America with Google Maps screenshots. I'm currently working on a project to find patterns of protein oxidation during the cell cycle and to build a model capable of predicting these patterns. Future plans include analysing some web traffic and marketing data from Brickborrow.it.

I like strategy games, tasting new food, playing tennis and basketball, and in a few weeks I will hike the West Highland Way in Scotland.
